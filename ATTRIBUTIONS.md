# Asset notes

The first three wallpapers were supplied for the Ankh theme and are included
as part of its visual identity. They were resized for 3840×2160 displays
without adding new artwork or changing the selected compositions.

`04-omarchy-wordmark.png` uses the official Omarchy wordmark from the
[Omarchy brand page](https://omarchy.org/brand/), recolored with the Ankh
palette and rendered at 3840×2160.

## Prévias

`preview.png` e `preview-unlock.png` são capturas reais deste tema aplicado
no Omarchy. Não foram adaptadas, recoloridas ou geradas por IA. O procedimento
